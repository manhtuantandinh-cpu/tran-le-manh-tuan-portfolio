The BA reasoning

A flow with explicit decisions and exceptions

The public rules establish the participation and reward conditions. The case-study process makes proposed system checks and human review visible at each handoff.

01 · PredictCustomer reviews the rules and submits a Long/Short choice within the daily window.
02 · Match dataPrice and eligible trading data are brought together for the same session.
03 · DecideDecision rules classify outcomes; incomplete inputs stay pending for investigation.
04 · ReviewOperations reviews a result snapshot before communication and payout handling.
